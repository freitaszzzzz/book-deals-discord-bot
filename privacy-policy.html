Privacy Policy

Book Deals Bot (“the Bot”) · Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information the Bot collects and how it is used. The Bot posts book, ebook and Kindle deals from RSS/Atom feeds into Discord servers.

1. Information we collect

The Bot stores only the data needed to work in your server:

  • Your Discord server (guild) ID and the ID of the channel chosen for deal posts;
  • The feed URLs, keywords and blocked words that server administrators configure;
  • A record of which deals have already been posted (anonymized identifiers of feed items), so the same deal is not posted twice.

2. Information we do not collect

  • The Bot does not read, store or process the content of messages in your server;
  • The Bot does not collect usernames, user IDs, avatars, e-mail addresses or any other personal data about server members;
  • The Bot does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

Commands are handled through Discord's slash command system. Discord itself processes the information you send when you use these commands, under Discord's Privacy Policy.

3. How the information is used

The data is used only to operate the Bot: fetching the feeds you configured, filtering the results and posting them in the selected channel. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties.

4. Third-party content

The Bot fetches content from feed URLs added by server administrators and posts titles, descriptions, images and links from those feeds. This content belongs to its respective owners, and the websites you are directed to have their own privacy policies.

5. Data retention and deletion

Records of posted deals are automatically deleted after 90 days. Server configuration is kept while the Bot is in use. Server administrators can remove feeds and clear keyword filters at any time with the Bot's commands, and may request complete deletion of their server's data by contacting us (see below).

6. Security

Data is stored on the server where the Bot runs and is not made public. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

7. Children

The Bot is intended for use on Discord, which requires users to meet its minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last changed.

9. Contact

For questions or data deletion requests, contact: jumentowork@gmail.com
